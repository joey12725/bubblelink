# Canonical facts

This is the one-page verified summary of BubbleLink's final design and status. Every other doc in this repo was checked against it. **If any doc disagrees with this page, this page wins.** Then fix the other doc.

Verified against the primary sources: the v1.2 release package, the AliExpress order history (2026-08-06), the final cart and decisions in the design thread, the v1.1 Release thread, the PCBWay emails, and the 2026-10-14 design review.

## Identity and status
- BubbleLink is Joey's **pack-fed, two-channel modular bubble system**. A backpack holds the battery, the 1 L bubble reservoir, one shared transfer pump, and an automatic pump controller PCB. Hand-held bubble heads (donor toy-gun mechanisms) plug in through umbilicals.
- **Joey's existing overvolted three-head bubble gun** (2S1P Samsung 25R, ~18 Wh) is real, working baseline hardware, called the "Equivalent Gun Unit" (EGU). It is NOT a completed BubbleLink prototype. It's the performance reference. (The the target festival guns are the dual-head pair below.)
- **Target event:** the target festival, **2026-10-30 to 31**. Project must be complete by **2026-10-29**.
- **As of 2026-10-14:** the AliExpress parts are on hand (Joey, 2026-08-21: all bubble parts on hand except the PCB). The PCBs shipped and are in transit: 4PX **[tracking removed]**, SpeedX **[tracking removed]**, customs cleared 10-12, ETA **Oct 15–18**. Board receipt and acceptance are not yet confirmed. Joey's estimate: 6–8 days of build after the boards arrive, if prep is done first.
- **CAD:** paused 2026-10-11 until purchase details were available, then lifted 2026-10-14. Printed-part CAD now exists in `hardware/printed/`: the pack is one upright "stick" (about 99.6 × 77.8 × 220 mm, 3.9 × 3.1 × 8.7 in) beside the bladder. **None of it has been printed or test-fitted yet.** Hardware (all from Joey's HVAKHVA DIN 912 stainless kit): 6 M3 socket-head screws (2× M3×10 for the cap, each with a flat washer over a TPU washer; 4× M3×12 for the cup, each with a split lock washer) + 6 M3 nuts glued into printed pockets (4 hex pockets dropped into the bulkhead from the dry side, 2 slots in the cap), zip ties, glue; no anaerobic threadlocker near ASA; TPU plugs (parts 39, 40) close the trimmer windows and USB-C port, so no tape; no foam (compliant TPU battery pads, pump mat and shim set, parts 36-38); rigid parts are ASA, seals and cushions TPU; 32 STLs (including four stick fit coupons); earlier "no screws" statements are superseded. Built for hours of jumping (2026-10-14 jostle pass): umbilical paracord loads go into U-tunnel bosses at the cup's gun ports, not the hose or solder joints; PRIME is guarded and hard-stopped; LED windows are glue-filled through-holes; the grommet has a silicone-filled top and a drip skirt, and the cup drains through whichever wall is down. **Not verified:** the cap seal (wet-test it), venting if a cell fails, and enclosed-charging temperature (charge with the cap off until the first enclosed charge has been checked). Details: [09-printed-parts.md](09-printed-parts.md).
- **Build order to 2026-10-29:** (1) now: check/buy the PCB THT parts (no purchase record), get written the target festival approval, bench-test Buck A (<5.25 V under load) and the pump (6 V marking, <0.3 A at 5 V); (2) print the coupon, cell holders and slide clamps, then the stick parts and bricks; (3) battery build; (4) boards arrive Oct 15–18: THT + bring-up; (5) gun teardown; (6) electronics into the sled; (7) fluid + umbilicals; (8) first fire + tuning; (9) bench gates; (10) done Oct 29.

- **Guns for the target festival:** a pair of dual-head guns (2 guns × 2 donor blower heads). Joey is picking up ~20 bubble guns the week of 2026-10-14 to strip for heads: pick 4 matched heads, keep spares. Each gun's two head motors run in parallel on its XT30U, and both donor pumps are fed from its umbilical through one assortment tee. Rough estimate (unmeasured): ~12 A with all 4 heads firing (~6 A per channel), about 2–2.5 h of heavy firing, about 2 h per 1 L. Gun/head mounts aren't designed yet; they need the teardown measurements.

## Baseline measurements (existing gun, 2S1P 25R)
- 2,500 mAh cells. ~15 min full-trigger life. ~70 W firing power (inferred). Continuous fluid use 118.4 mL / 7 min ≈ 16.9 mL/min. Heavy event 473.6 mL in ~75 min, giving ~37% duty, ~26 W average, ~0.069 Wh/mL.
- Joey's batteries get warm, once nearly too hot to touch, under heavy use. The gun's spring contacts are poor ("hot garbage"), so solder the connections.
- Field history: a summer festival 2026-08-02 (five canisters in 80–90 min); a convention 2026-09-04 to 06 (venue staff refused the gun at Friday's EDM event despite earlier approval).

## Power (final)
- **Battery: 2S5P**, 10× Samsung INR18650-25R (owned; match and qualify first). 7.2 V nominal, 8.4 V full, 12.5 Ah, **90 Wh**. Cells 18.33 ± 0.07 mm × 64.85 ± 0.15 mm. Printed 5×2 offset cell holders (MID and TERM, 19.9 mm pitch, 0.8 mm lip, block 113.0 × 40.68 × 67.45 mm), fish-paper insulator rings, **nickel-plated steel tabs** (3 × 10 pcs, 2.6 cm; not pure nickel), with **five parallel series bridges** so no single strip carries full pack current. Spot welded. The two rows face **opposite** directions (row A + one way, row B − that way); if every cell faces the same way, it's 10P, not 2S5P. It's a block on the stick's sled, not a separate cartridge.
- **BMS:** IC GOGOGO 2S **8.4 V 40 A** Li-ion with balance.
- **Charger:** SZHJW USB-C → 2S 8.4 V boost charger, **2S 4A** variant. Charging is battery-side.
- **Main protection:** ONE **30 A manual-reset breaker** (YONGRUN Type B-30A, 12–48 V DC, **M6 threaded studs**). Measured 2026-10-14: 1⅝ in square plate, 4 in (102 mm) long, 1⅝ in thick, mounting holes 1¼ in apart (the listing's 72×48×44 mm was wrong; it's longer but slimmer). It's an inline type: studs at both ends, with a red reset lever and a red push-to-trip button on the square plate. In the stick it stands upright behind the sled's cross panel, 2 mm from the outer wall, hung by zip ties through its plate holes and tall slots in the spine. The tube walls are solid (the breaker window and TPU membrane were removed 2026-10-14 as the stick's biggest leak risk), so its controls are only reachable with the sled out. **To reset it:** cap off (2 screws), lift the sled out (leave enough wiring slack), reset the lever. A trip means a real fault (a short, a jammed motor); find the cause before resetting. It's in the **+ line only**. Wire to the studs with **SC10-6 copper ring lugs**, solder-filled and strain-relieved (the 10 mm² barrel is oversized for 12 AWG). Never solder to the studs. There are no per-channel fuses.
- **Wire:** **12 AWG** silicone (black/red, 3 m each) for the battery/main path; **16 AWG** (black/red, 5 m each) for gun branches/umbilicals.
- **Connectors:** **XT60H** for the power key (an XT60H male socket on the cap top and an XT60H female key); **XT30U** (AMASS-style, 10 pairs) per gun channel, power + and − only, with no signal wire to the guns (power always comes from a female: pack side is the female in a well on the cup's outer wall; the umbilical's pack end is the male and its gun end is the female; the gun's input is the male); **JST-XH** kit for low-current/PCB/balance.
- **High-current path:** `battery → BMS → power key (XT60H) → 30 A breaker → ACS758 high-current terminals → split → two XT30U gun channels`. Gun current never passes through the PCB.
- **Current sensor:** ACS758LCB-**050B** module, 5 V supply, **40 mV/A**, ~2.5 V at zero current, ±50 A. It measures **combined** gun current, not per-channel.
- **Gun bus:** raw, unregulated 2S (~6.0–8.4 V). Fixed full-power triggers. No per-gun PWM.
- **Printed-part fit (lid and sled, Joey, 2026-10-16):** the voltmeter hole is 31.2 mm (29.2 mm printed too small); the USB-C charger sits in a ~4.1 mm slot (its thin top end), not a full-thickness pocket; a sled printed before 2026-10-16 needs its front-right corner cut off to clear the Buck B shelf.
- **Printed-part fit (coupons, Joey, 2026-10-15):** `cell_bore` = 18.65 mm (the .65 ring fits his 25R cells in ASA); M3 nut pockets, the XT30U well and the XT60H key socket verified; hose hole 6.0 mm.
- **5 V rails:** two YxPC 6–20 V → 5 V USB bucks. **Buck A** powers the controller PCB and pump (~5.0–5.1 V; the PCB's qualified input is 4.75–5.25 V). **Buck B** powers the LED backpack's USB. The listing has no defensible current spec. Buck A is soldered straight to PCB J1; Buck B has no output wiring, since the backpack plugs into its own USB-A port through a window in the tube's outer wall (Buck B stands on a shelf on the tube's front wall, with its input run from the sled on a 2-pin JST-XH plug; measure Buck B before printing the tube, since the shelf assumes 25.4 × 17 × 8 mm). If Buck A misbehaves, the recommended external upgrade is a **Pololu S13V30F5** (no PCB change).
- **Voltmeter:** Stlxy DC 4–100 V two-wire round LED voltmeter, red, 5 bought (1 used, 4 spares). It's the battery gauge; there's no percentage fuel gauge. Wire it **after the breaker**. Its idle draw is unmeasured (small LED meters typically draw tens of mA); measure it once with a multimeter.
- **Turning it off:** pulling the **power key** (the XT60H key in the KEY socket on the cap top) is the OFF switch: it turns off everything downstream (breaker, ACS758, bucks, PCB, voltmeter, pump, guns). **It is not a full battery disconnect:** the battery stays connected to the BMS, the charger and one pin of the key socket. For storage or transport, key out and the TPU key blank in. The charger connects upstream of the key, so it works with the key out. The BMS quiescent draw is unmeasured, typically tiny. Electrical work requires disconnecting the battery and the USB-C input.
- **Measured parts (Joey, tape measure, 2026-10-14):** BMS 2½ × 1⅞ × ¼ in; bucks 1 in along the port × 5/16 in thick (width not measured; 17 mm assumed); ACS758 module 1½ × ~¾ × ⅛ in; USB-C charger 1½ × 11/16 × ⅜ in (receptacle centred on the 17.5 mm short side, confirmed 2026-10-15); voltmeter bezel ~1 5/16 in, body a hair over 1⅛ in, about 1 in deep or a bit more.

## Fluid (final)
- **Reservoir:** UWWWMM **1 L** blue hydration bladder with a **101 cm detachable drinking tube** (keep its leakproof disconnect; cut the tube short).
- **Pump:** ONE **GROTHEN G528** peristaltic, **6 V, 3×5 mm tube, 150 mL/min** variant (4–6 V rated, ~0.25–0.30 A). It runs from Buck A's 5 V through the PCB's MOSFET. Check that the delivered motor is marked 6 V (the seller art says 12 V). It's shared by both channels. Measured 2026-10-14: 2 × 1¾ × 1½ in at its thickest (the earlier 60×60×50 / 65×55×50 mm figures were wrong).
- **Tubing:** food-grade transparent silicone, **3.5 mm ID × 5.5 mm OD, 5 m**. This is the ONLY system tubing; there's no 6 mm trunk. The pump's own tube is 3×5 mm.
- **Fittings:** the black nylon **car-hose connector assortment** (set) supplies the unions, tees, and reducers. Use `#68401` 1/8" straight unions for the pump and umbilical bricks, and `#68409` 5/32" (≈4 mm) tees (or `#68408` 1/8") for the manifold. The 1/4" and 3/8" reducers mate the bladder's factory hose to the 3.5 mm silicone. Secure barbs with narrow zip ties; warm silicone in hot water to stretch it.
- **Check valve:** BigBigBear **4 mm** one-way, **0.003 MPa (0.435 psi) opening pressure**, PP body, silicone core, 304 SS spring, liquid-rated, hose barb. One only (the listing is limited to 1 per shopper).
- **Recirculating bypass:** `outlet tee 1 side branch → printed slide clamp (restrictor) → check valve → pump-inlet tee`, valve arrow toward the inlet/reservoir side. Excess flow recirculates instead of force-feeding a stopped donor pump, and the reservoir can't siphon past the stopped pump.
- **Path:** `1 L bladder → factory tube (cut short) → assortment reducer → 3.5×5.5 silicone → inlet tee (bypass return) → G528 → outlet tee 1 (bypass branch) → outlet tee 2 → gun 1 / gun 2`. **Three tees** in total.
- Unused channels are closed with **printed slide clamps** (no purchased shutoff valves). One slide-clamp design serves as the bypass restrictor and both channel shutoffs. The fluid hose is **semi-permanent** for the MVP; the XT30U remains the disconnect.
- Donor pumps stay in place as the final metering stage. Donor-side reducers get chosen only after measuring each donor's tubing.
- **Umbilical length:** 4 ft standard, 6 ft experimental. Head may be ~5 ft above the pack.
- **Solution:** JOYFY 32 oz concentrate ("up to 2.5 gallons"), commercial concentrate first. Liquid Germall Plus 100 g was also bought as a preservative. Rinse/flush, drain, and dry after every event; never store solution in the bladder.
- Future fluid profiles to test (intentions only): Lulu 9:1 or JOYIN 10:1 water:concentrate; outdoor stream matrix of 1,000 mL water + 50–70 mL Dawn Professional + 0–0.20 g J-Lube + 0–10 mL glycerin; soluble fluorescent dye only after residue tests.

## Controller PCB: BubbleLink CTRL v1.2 (final)
- Purpose: an **automatic transfer-pump controller**. When either gun draws current, the pump runs; when the guns stop, the pump keeps running for a calibrated **~350 ms** off-delay (range ~35–574 ms), then stops.
- U1 **TI TLV1822QDRQ1** dual comparator (SOIC-8; v1.2 land pattern per TI D0008A, 5.40 mm row centers, 1.27 mm pitch). Q2 **AO3400A** low-side pump switch. Q1/Q3 **DMP2065UQ-7**. D1 **B340A** flyback. F1 **1812L150/16DR** PTC. U2 **TLV803EA43DBZR** supervisor (4.38 V, ~200 ms). FB1 ferrite, logic-rail filtering. R18 pulls ACS_OUT low (unplugged sensor = off). R1/C5 10 ms sensor filter.
- RV1 (2 kΩ 3296W) sets the detection threshold, ~2.21–2.80 V; hysteresis ~4.42 mV. RV2 (100 kΩ 3296W) sets the off-delay, calibrated to 350 ms.
- LEDs: LED1 green = power, LED2 yellow = gun active, LED3 red = pump active (Kingbright L-934GD/YD/ID). SW1 Omron B3F-1000 = **PRIME** (momentary).
- **J4 AUTO/FORCE-ON input** is DNP: a no-respin fallback if the ACS758 can't detect a very low-current or PWM-slowed head. **R22 DNP. C9 and R18 fitted.**
- 41 top-side SMT placements, 92 paste apertures, no bottom-side parts. User-soldered THT: J1, J3 (B2B-XH), J2 (B3B-XH), C1 (Rubycon 16ZLH100MEFC5X11 100 µF 16 V), RV1, RV2, SW1, LED1–3.
- External connections only: 5 V + GND from Buck A; 3 wires to the ACS758; 2 wires to the pump.
- The PCB does NOT do charging, BMS, main overcurrent, gun regulation/PWM, per-channel control, fuel gauge, voltmeter, backpack power, or firmware.
- Release QA: 50 PASS / 0 FAIL. Electrical Monte Carlo of 20,000 samples passed. **Nine bench-only gates remain pending** (no physical measurements claimed).

## Carrier and cosplay (final)
- The bag is the MAX LED **"M Style" Bluetooth LED pixel backpack**. Its internal geometry is unknown until measured (the "~34 × 20 × 7 cm" figure is an AI estimate of the exterior from photos, not an internal measurement). Buck B powers its display (target 2–3 W average, 4 W ceiling).
- Layout: the printed **stick** stands upright beside the bladder, back against the wearer's back. Battery high in the dry tube; the draining wet cup at the bottom. Nothing exits underneath: both umbilicals plug into gun ports on the cup's outer wall, the bladder hose enters at the IN pigtail on the front wall, the LED backpack plugs into a USB-A window in the tube wall, and the power key and charger are on the cap top. Ports: CHG, KEY, LED 5V, 1, 2, IN. See [09-printed-parts.md](09-printed-parts.md).
- **Inputs and outputs of the stick** (nothing exits underneath; it stands on its floor): **CHG** (USB-C under a TPU plug, cap top), **KEY** (XT60H male socket on the cap top, with the XT60H female power key; key in = ON, key out = all loads off with the battery still on the BMS, the charger and one socket pin, charger upstream of the key; key blank in for storage), **LED 5V** (Buck B's own USB-A receptacle in a 14.0 × 6.6 mm window in the tube's outer wall, TPU plug in the counterbore), **1** and **2** (gun ports on the cup's outer wall: XT30U female well, hose pigtail, paracord U-tunnel, drip hood, TPU port cap when unplugged), **IN** (bladder hose pigtail on the cup's front wall), and drains (floor and every wall). The bulkhead grommet passes only the two 16 AWG gun pairs and the two pump leads. Details: [hardware/printed/README.md](../hardware/printed/README.md).
- **Tracer (Overwatch) cosplay** is a separate project that can share the pack. Hollow printed gun shells with bubble guns installed, everything printed in white, a 2 m ice-blue 5 V COB LED strip (not waterproof, not UV). Printers: Creality K2 Plus (350³ mm) and Bambu Lab A1 (256³ mm).

## More things that are not part of the system
- No "removable cassette" or QD cassette. Inside the MAX LED backpack there's one printed upright **stick**: a sealed dry tube (battery + electronics on a slide-out sled, with a control-head cap) over a draining wet cup (pump and fittings), next to the bladder.
- Superseded printed-part concepts (don't design or print): the separate battery cartridge, wet/dry divider + electronics tray, PCB mount bracket, buck pads, breaker bracket/fence, voltmeter faceplate, control-panel faceplate, pump cradle, pump/hose/tether block, the Cults3D spacer remix (replaced by the original 5×2 offset cell holders MID/TERM, 19.9 mm pitch), and the intermediate "dry core" wide box (~157 × 171 × 52 mm). Still in use: the fit coupon, the MID/TERM cell holders, the slide clamps ×3 and the umbilical bricks ×4.
- The breaker is in the **+ line only**; no black (−) wire goes to it. The PCB has **no fused XT30U outputs** (there are no per-channel fuses). The pump's 3×5 tube is **3 mm ID × 5 mm OD**. RV1/RV2 are **25-turn** 3296W trimmers. The PCB hole pattern is **43 × 33 mm**.
- No 7 L festival pack or "club bag" migration. No an earlier festival bag rules (an older event; they don't apply to the target festival).
- No pump mode switch / OFF-AUTO-ON switch. The PCB has only the momentary **PRIME** button (SW1) and the DNP **J4 AUTO/FORCE-ON** input.
- No 40 A backup/one-shot fuse. Protection is the single 30 A breaker, plus F1 (1.5 A PTC, 1812L150/16DR) on the PCB pump branch.
- The PCB comparator is the TLV1822QDRQ1, NOT an LM393. The flyback diode is the B340A, NOT an SS34. Q1/Q3 are DMP2065UQ-7 P-channel parts, NOT BSS84. PRIME grounds DET_N and runs the same delay path; it doesn't drive Q2 directly.
- Fluid terminations use nylon barbs from the car-hose assortment with narrow zip ties, NOT Luer-lock barbs or hose clamps.
- C1 is 100 µF 16 V (Rubycon 16ZLH100MEFC5X11), NOT 470 µF.
