Plug map: what connects to what

Every connection is drawn as one line, whether it's two wires or three. The chip on each line says how it joins: XT30 for power-only runs, JST-XH for everything off the control board, XT60H where the full gun current flows, solder where two parts are joined for good. purple chips are the ports on the outside of the case.

Ports on each component

Build each part's leads from this list, then plug things together at the end. The plug-only components are listed first, then the ones with soldered or clamp connections. Every plug pairs a male with a female of the same type. The rule: whichever side stays live when unplugged gets the female. On JST, you soldered wires straight onto the board's J1, J2 and J3 pads and ended each in a female plug, so whatever they plug into is male: Buck A's output lead, the pump lead and the ACS758's pin header. There are no cables in between. Solder joints and clamps have no male or female.

How to read the chips

Each line has exactly one chip: the plug where those two parts come apart, or solder where they're joined for good. A part's own soldered leads aren't drawn separately. For example, Buck A's two output wires are part of Buck A, and the line to the PCB is just the JST-XH that goes into J1.

On XT30 and XT60H, the side that stays live when unplugged gets the female. On JST, your board's wires end in female plugs, so what they plug into is male.

Connector count

  • XT30: all 10 of your 10 pairs. In the case: charger, voltmeter, Buck A, Buck B and the two gun plugs (6). Gun ports: 2 females in the wells plus 2 males on the umbilicals. Umbilical gun ends: 2 pairs. There's no spare, so solder carefully.
  • XT60H: 2 of 10. The key socket with the key, and the sensor.
  • JST-XH: the board's three female plugs are already done. You need two male 2-pin plugs from the kit: Buck A's output and the pump. The ACS758 uses its own header pins.

Taking it apart

  • Cap off: pull J1, J2 and J3, and unplug the charger and the voltmeter. The two key wires stay connected, so the cap sits beside the stick on that tether.
  • Sled out: also unplug the two gun XT30s at the bulkhead and Buck B's XT30, and unplug the backpack from LED 5V first. The pump lead stays with the pump and slides out of the grommet slit.
