Building the 2S5P battery block

10 Samsung 25R cells, two printed holders (MID and TERM), nickel tabs on both faces. Step through it, or press Play. The geometry matches the holders: 19.9 mm between neighbouring cells, 26 mm tabs.

pack − bus (row A, TERM face) pack + bus (row B, TERM face) midpoint mesh (MID face) fish paper

Soldering, if you're not spot-welding

Flux, an iron of 80 W or more with a big tip, pre-tinned tabs. Under 3 seconds per joint on a cell, then let that cell cool. Keep the iron on the tab, not the holder: ASA softens around 100 °C.

The two meter checks

After the TERM face, the two chains read 3.6–4.2 V apart (0 V means a tab bridges them). After the MID face they read 7.2–8.4 V (about 4 V means a row is in backwards).

Tabs

Every pair of neighbouring cells is 19.9 mm apart, in a row or across the rows, so each 26 mm tab bridges exactly one pair. TERM gets 8 tabs, MID gets 13: 21 of your 30.
